This was my first engine and the foundation for the graphics work that followed.
It rendered microelectronic structures interactively in 3D, and I presented it
at the National Research University of Electronic Technology (MIET) in 2012. A
C++/OpenGL flight simulator was built alongside it on the same foundations.

The rendering pipeline was implemented directly rather than assembled from a
framework: GLSL shader compilation, GPU vertex and index buffers, textures,
framebuffers and render-target handling, geometry submission and draw-call
management. Writing that layer by hand is where the habits came from: being
specific about how data reaches the GPU, and what the cost of each frame
actually is.

The project is complete and belongs to 2012. The engine I am building now,
[Radiance4D](/projects/radiance4d/), is new work rather than a continuation of
this one.
