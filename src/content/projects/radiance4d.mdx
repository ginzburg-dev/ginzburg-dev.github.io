Radiance4D is my current graphics engine project: a hybrid real-time renderer
built around spatiotemporal reconstruction, written in C++20 against Direct3D 12
with HLSL shaders.

It pulls together the two strands I have worked on longest. Real-time rendering
goes back to writing my own OpenGL engine and later production work in Unreal
Engine; spatiotemporal reconstruction is the same problem as temporal denoising,
approached from the renderer's side rather than from post-processing, using
information across frames to produce an image that is both cheaper and steadier
than any single frame can be.
